A cafe built around books

The Cafe Library mixes a reading room with a cafe. Wooden shelves reach the ceiling, warm lights run along every row, and the tables sit right beside the books.

  • Shelves of books you can pick up and read at your table
  • Shakes, coffee, mojitos, burgers, fries and pasta
  • Tables that work for a laptop and a dessert
  • A small stage with a mic and guitars
  • Board games like Jenga

Come as you are

Bring a friend, a laptop or just yourself. Find us on the map and walk in.
